# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Two crew members on a long mission (Mars surface, Moon base, ISS), one examining the other. The examiner holds a phone, often one-handed, sometimes while holding a light to an eye or steadying a crewmate. Tired, possibly worried, in a cramped habitat under mixed artificial light. Secondary: hackathon judges seeing it for 3 minutes on a mirrored phone screen.

## Product Purpose
A five-minute peer health exam that works with no internet. Phone sensors take the measurements, each person is compared with their own past exams, and every flag ends with what to do now and when to call the flight surgeon (NASA ISS Medical Checklist). Success: a first-time user always knows the next step without asking.

## Brand Personality
Calm, plain, trustworthy. A good medical checklist, not a game or a sci-fi HUD. Owner's words: "make the UI/UX usable, not super flashy, but at least I should know what to do"; "not AI slop looking"; "I don't want any more higher stuff for UI/UX".

## Anti-references
- Generic AI app look: gradient heroes, glowing neon accents, glassmorphism, icon-in-a-circle card grids, badge chips everywhere, emoji in UI.
- Sci-fi HUD dressing (scanlines, corner brackets, fake telemetry) that adds nothing to the task.
- Debug views shown to users (the red-pixel pupil view confused the owner).

## Design Principles
1. The next action is always the biggest thing on screen, at the bottom, reachable with a thumb.
2. One instruction at a time; say who does it (examiner or subject).
3. Every test ends with a plain verdict: looks normal, worth a recheck, couldn't measure.
4. Numbers are always shown next to the person's usual value.
5. Familiar product UI beats novelty. Decoration only where it carries state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable in dim habitats and bright rooms: body text at least 16 px, contrast WCAG AA, touch targets at least 44 px. Works without sound (voice is optional) and without colour alone (flags also use text). Eyes-closed steps use beeps and vibration.
