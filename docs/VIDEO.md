# 30-second demo video

Rules from the submission guide: 30 s max, no NASA logos, nothing fabricated. Film on the phone in airplane mode so the icon is in the status bar the whole time.

## Setup (10 min, before any filming)
- Two phones: phone A runs the app, phone B films. Or scrcpy on the laptop and screen-record.
- Phone A: open https://second-opinion-ruddy.vercel.app, tap Try demo, run one exam once so the camera and sensor permissions are already granted. Then airplane mode ON. Brightness max.
- Lighting: a window or a desk lamp on the face, no light behind. Dark eyes need the flash, which the app does.
- Record every clip 2 to 3 times. Cut in CapCut or the phone's editor. Captions on, no music louder than the voice.

## Shot list and voice-over

| Time | Shot | Voice-over (read at normal pace, 70 words total) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | Black. White text: "Mars. A message home takes 11 minutes. There is no doctor." | "On a long mission there is no doctor and the signal is minutes away." |
| 4-8 s | Two people at a table, one holds the phone to the other's eye. Flash fires. Screen shows the pupil curve drawing. | "So your crewmate examines you. The camera times your pupil." |
| 8-13 s | Close on fingertip over the back camera. Pulse wave scrolls, bpm settles. Phone visibly buzzes in the hand. | "The camera reads your pulse. You feel it in your hand." |
| 13-17 s | Phone flat on the chest, eyes closed. Beeps. Verdict "Looks normal". | "The motion sensor measures your balance." |
| 17-24 s | Screen record: result card. Three flags, scroll to "Do now", then the Mars delay line and "Queue for the next comms window". | "Everything is compared with your own baseline. Every flag maps to NASA's medical checklist. Then it queues a 150-character note for the surgeon." |
| 24-28 s | Crew board on the home screen, then the QR code looping, second phone receiving "2 of 3". | "Records move phone to phone with no network." |
| 28-30 s | Title card: "Second Opinion" and under it "Works with no internet. Team Vortexx." Zoom on the airplane icon in the status bar. | "Second Opinion." |

## Phone-screen clips that always work
If the live sensors misbehave on camera, these are the safe takes and they are still honest:
- Result card: home → Try demo → tap Asha's latest exam. Scroll slowly.
- Pupil curve: /lab/pupil in a lit room. The curve appears whether or not the reading is clean.
- Pulse wave: /lab/hr. Keep the finger light. The wave draws from the first second.
- Mars line and countdown: bottom of any result card.
- QR loop: home → "Share or receive records with another phone".
